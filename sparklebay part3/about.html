About SparkleBay

Established in 2024 by two young entrepreneurs who began washing vehicles at customers' homes on weekends, SparkleBay now serves the whole community.

Our Mission

To provide convenient, affordable and environmentally responsible vehicle cleaning services with reliable customer service and care.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted neighborhood car wash brand, and to grow into a small fleet service provider supporting local businesses on the road.

Our Values

  • Reliability — We show up when we say we will.
  • Care — Every vehicle is treated like our own.
  • Honesty — Clear pricing, no surprises.
  • Responsibility — Mindful water use and eco-friendly products wherever possible.

Who We Serve

  • Students
  • Busy Professionals
  • Small Business Fleets
  • Car Enthusiasts
